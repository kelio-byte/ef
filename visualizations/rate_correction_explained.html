The Fix — One Character Changed

In edit_flows/sampling/euler.py, the rate correction divides k_sample by k_train. Cubic's derivative is 0 at t=0, so k_train = 0. The old clamp_min(1e-12) let the ratio reach 1 trillion. Changing to clamp_min(1e-2) caps it at 100×, which is harmless since the model outputs near-zero rates at t=0 anyway (no training signal there under cubic).
